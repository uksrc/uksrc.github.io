**In development — details may change.** The pilot will start by March 2027 at the latest, launched through a [science support programme](/science-projects/) call.
{: .notice--warning}

The specialist user support pilot offers **short, focused blocks of hands-on help from ukSRC specialists**, to get you past the technical hurdles between your data and your science.

## What we will offer

| | What we will offer | What it isn't |
|---|---|---|
| **The help** | Advice and hands-on work alongside you — for example getting a pipeline running or speeding up code | A finished product delivered for you |
| **Time** | Fixed blocks of roughly one to four weeks | Open-ended or embedded support |
| **Expertise** | Areas listed with each call. Examples could include low-frequency pipelines, VLBI, transients and GPU porting | Any skill on request |
| **Who** | Projects on the science support programme or using ukSRC software | Fast-track projects (compute only) |

The kind of help available includes:

- getting a pipeline running on your data;
- analysing data, or speeding up code (e.g. porting to GPUs);
- containerising a workflow so others can reuse it.

## How it will work

1. **Expertise** — we publish the expertise on offer with each quarterly science support programme call.
2. **You ask** — tick a box on your application and say what you need.
3. **We match** — we check it's feasible, agree the scope and pair you with a specialist.
4. **Focused block** — a specialist works with you for a fixed block of time during the quarter.
5. **You tell us** — short feedback that shapes the next call.

### What we ask of you

- A specific request with a realistic timescale.
- Be available during your block, then complete a 10-minute questionnaire.
- Acknowledge or include the specialist on resulting papers.

## Timeline

- **Before the call** — we finalise the pilot: process, application questions and our skills map.
- **Call opens** — areas on offer are published alongside a science support programme call.
- **Next quarter** — first blocks run, after onboarding.
- **After round one** — we review and adjust, using feedback from projects and specialists.
