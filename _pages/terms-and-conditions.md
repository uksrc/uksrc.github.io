By filling the registration form, users agree to work on the computing servers under the conditions described in this form and in this document.

## 1. Account security and confidentiality

**1.1 Personal accounts:** Accounts created on ukSRC computing servers are individual and personal and must not be shared or transferred to others.

**1.2 Data and password security:** Although ukSRC makes every effort to ensure data security, users are responsible for the security and confidentiality of their data and account credentials, including SSH keys and passwords.

## 2. Acceptable use of computing resources

**2.1 Lawful use:** Users must use ukSRC computing facilities only for authorised, lawful purposes consistent with ukSRC policies. Fraud, harassment, unauthorised commercial activities, copyright infringement, and other prohibited activities are not permitted.

**2.2 Responsible resource use:** Users must use CPU time, memory, storage, network bandwidth, accelerators, and other computing resources responsibly. They must comply with resource quotas, job scheduling rules, usage limits, and allocation policies, and must not disrupt services or other users.

**2.3 Software and system integrity:** Users must use properly licensed and authorised software. They must not install unauthorised software, alter system configurations, bypass technical restrictions, or attempt to gain elevated privileges without permission.

**2.4 Compliance with provider and home institution policies:** Users must comply with the applicable policies, terms and conditions, and acceptable-use requirements of the underlying service providers and their home institutions. Users granted access to University of Cambridge cloud resources must also follow the University of Cambridge cloud terms. Users granted access to the ukSRC COSMA resources must also follow Durham University's COSMA policies.

## 3. Data management and security incident reporting

**3.1 Data management:** Users are responsible for managing their files and research data appropriately, including complying with applicable storage, backup, retention, classification, and deletion requirements. Users should not assume that all data is automatically backed up.

**3.2 Incident reporting:** Software malfunctions, suspected malware, unauthorised access, data loss, and other security incidents must be reported promptly through the designated [ukSRC helpdesk](https://uksrc.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portals){:target="_blank"}.

## 4. Operational requirements and continued access

**4.1 Maintenance and service procedures:** Users must comply with published maintenance schedules, access restrictions, and service-specific operating procedures, and must avoid activities that interfere with maintenance or essential system operations.

**4.2 Development status, shared risk:** The ukSRC computing facilities are still under development, and users acknowledge that services may be subject to changes, limitations, or disruptions. Use of the facilities involves shared risks, and users are responsible for managing and safeguarding their data appropriately.

**4.3 Periodic check-ins:** Users must respond to periodic ukSRC check-ins within the specified deadline to confirm their continued need for computing resources. Failure to respond after reminders may result in suspension or termination of access to ukSRC computing facilities.

## 5. Acknowledgement of ukSRC support in publications

**5.1 Publication acknowledgement:** Papers and publications resulting from the use of ukSRC facilities must include the following acknowledgement:

> This work has been enabled by access to facilities and the scientific and technical support provided by the UK SKA Regional Centre (ukSRC). The ukSRC is a collaboration between the University of Cambridge, University of Edinburgh, Durham University, University of Hertfordshire, University of Manchester, University College London, the UKRI STFC Scientific Computing (STFC) at RAL, and RAL Space. The ukSRC is supported by funding from the UKRI STFC.

## 6. Access restrictions and enforcement

**6.1 Denial or withdrawal of access:** ukSRC reserves the right to deny, suspend, or withdraw access to its facilities where a user may endanger the security of computer systems or the service itself, in accordance with applicable policies and procedures.
