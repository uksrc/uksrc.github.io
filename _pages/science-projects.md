{: .notice--info}

The UK SKA Regional Centre (ukSRC) science support programme is a streamlined, year-round route for the UK radio astronomy community to access ukSRC resources and national computing infrastructure for their scientific research. It provides compute, storage, training and software help for SKAO-related science, including work with SKA precursors and pathfinders.

The programme is a key part of how the ukSRC consults, co-designs and co-develops a ukSRC node that is usable and widely adopted by the community. It supports evolving science and new technical innovations, preparing a ukSRC fit for the coming decades of SKA science.

## At a glance

- **Apply at any time** with a short, light-touch application.
- **New projects onboarded every quarter**, with basic HPC training for each round.
- **Resources across up to three UK sites**, with new capacity coming online across our partner sites every few months.
- **Fast-track director's discretionary time** for urgent, time-critical work, such as data that needs to be calibrated quickly.
- **Ongoing support**: a helpdesk and knowledge base, quarterly check-ins and HPC training, plus a [specialist user support pilot](/specialist-user-support/) offering hands-on help.

## What you get

Successful applicants will have access to:

- **ukSRC compute and storage resources** on national computing infrastructure.
- **Usable scientific workflows** within the ukSRC architecture.
- **Science, development and technical support** from the ukSRC team through the [ukSRC helpdesk](https://uksrc.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portals){:target="_blank"} and knowledge base.
- **Regular check-ins** with the ukSRC team each quarter.
- **Regular basic and advanced High Performance Computing (HPC) training** sessions. Basic training comes with each onboarding round and advanced training is offered during the year.
- **Specialist user support** (starting by March 2027 at the latest): short, focused blocks of one to four weeks of hands-on help from ukSRC specialists, for example getting a pipeline running on your data, speeding up or GPU-porting code, or containerising a workflow. You request it by ticking a box on your application. [Find out more](/specialist-user-support/).
- **Opportunities to contribute** to the future direction and features of the ukSRC and the SKA Regional Centre Network (SRCNet). Feedback from projects goes directly into how we develop and plan our services.

## Who can apply

- We welcome applications from **all astronomy and astrophysics fields**, including projects using SKA precursors and pathfinders.
- Projects may involve international users, but the **PI must be affiliated with a UK institute**.

## Ways to get time

### Quarterly onboarding rounds

The main route is rolling: applications are accepted throughout the year and assessed in batches. Selected projects are onboarded in quarterly rounds. To be considered for a round, submit your application by its deadline:

| Onboarding round | Application deadline |
|---|---|
| January 2027 | 1 December 2026 |
| March 2027 | 1 March 2027 |
| June 2027 | 1 June 2027 |
| September 2027 | 1 September 2027 |

Applications received after a deadline are automatically considered for the next onboarding round.

### Fast-track: director's discretionary time

Director's discretionary time (DDT) is available **year-round for urgent, time-critical projects** that can't wait for the next onboarding round, for example **data that needs to be calibrated quickly**. DDT requests receive a light assessment and expedited onboarding. DDT allocations provide compute only. To get specialist user support, apply through a quarterly round.

## A year on the programme

| | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Every quarter** | New projects onboarded with basic HPC training; check-ins with existing projects |
| **During the year** | Advanced HPC training |
| **Each January** | Annual PI report and renewal |

## How to apply

Complete the [application form](#){: .btn .btn--primary}. Once we have received your application, a member of the ukSRC team will contact you about the next steps.

## How applications are assessed

We aim to support as many projects as possible. The number of projects we can support depends on the resources available.

Applications receive a **light-touch assessment** of the resources requested against current ukSRC availability, mainly based on **data storage requirements**. The scientific objectives and merit of applications are **not** assessed.

Successful applications are granted resources for **up to 12 months**. Projects can be extended through annual user renewal and project reporting.

## Getting ready for SKA science verification

SKAO science verification (SV) begins in 2027. Projects on the programme will already be set up on ukSRC resources and familiar with our services when SV calls open. The ukSRC also plans a dedicated call for UK-led SV projects on national resources. See [science verification support](/science-verification/).

**Already running a ukSRC demonstrator case?** To continue after December 2026, submit an application to this programme. Projects that apply will move across at the January 2027 renewal.
