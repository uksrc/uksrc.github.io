The UK SKAO Science Committee Town Hall took place at the University of Manchester on 27–28 October 2026, bringing together members of the UK SKA community to share updates and discuss science using the SKA Observatory (SKAO), its pathfinders and precursors.

The two-day meeting featured updates from SKAO, a dedicated session on the UK Science Regional Centre (UKSRC), discussions on upcoming Science Verification observations and the Key Science Projects (KSP) process, alongside science highlight presentations from Early Career Researchers (ECRs).

Discussions provided an opportunity to exchange ideas, share recent scientific developments and explore the scientific and technical priorities shaping the next stages of the SKA programme.

The Town Hall also provided an opportunity to strengthen connections across the UK SKA community and support ongoing collaboration as preparations for the SKA Observatory continue.
