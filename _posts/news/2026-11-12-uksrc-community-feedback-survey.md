ukSRC invites researchers across the UK who are involved in or interested in SKA-related science, at all career stages, to take part in its Community Feedback Survey.

The survey aims to gather views from the wider research community to help the ukSRC better understand its needs, priorities and experiences. The feedback will inform the future development of ukSRC activities, support and community engagement.

Whether you are already involved in the ukSRC or have an interest in SKA-related science, your perspective is valuable. We encourage researchers to take a few minutes to complete the survey.
